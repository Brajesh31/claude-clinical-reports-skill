# Clinical Reports

A structured writing and validation skill for clinical and healthcare documentation.

## Supported report types

- Case reports aligned with CARE reporting guidance.
- Diagnostic reports for radiology, pathology, and laboratory workflows.
- Clinical trial documentation, including ICH-E3 style reports and safety reporting.
- Patient documentation such as SOAP notes, histories and physicals, and discharge summaries.

## Capabilities

- Apply structured templates and report conventions.
- Organize clinical findings into clear, objective sections.
- Support regulatory and quality considerations, including HIPAA, FDA, and ICH-GCP topics.
- Use included assets, references, and scripts to support drafting and validation.

## Repository contents

Alongside `SKILL.md`, this repository contains an implementation summary, templates and other assets, reference materials, and scripts. Review the relevant files before using a report workflow.

## Responsible use

Generated reports require review by qualified healthcare professionals. Verify every clinical fact, result, date, citation, and interpretation. Do not include identifiable patient information unless the environment and workflow are authorized to handle it, and follow applicable privacy, institutional, and regulatory requirements.
